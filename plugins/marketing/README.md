# Marketing

Plug-ins to plan, write, and ship content that compounds — from SEO and social to brand and lifecycle.

## When to install

You're running marketing for a product or service and want to:

- Build a content engine that doesn't depend on a copywriter for every asset
- Generate SEO-ready headlines, briefs, and outlines without the AI smell
- Keep tone and brand consistent across long-form, social, and lifecycle
- Move from "post and pray" to a measurable content strategy

## Categories

| Category | What lives here |
|---|---|
| `content` | Long-form, briefs, editorial calendars, repurposing |
| `seo` | Keyword research, headlines, on-page optimisation |
| `social` | LinkedIn, X, Instagram, threads, hooks |
| `brand` | Voice guides, positioning, messaging frameworks |

## Skills

**Free**
1. [Content Strategist](./skills/aipowered-content-strategist/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-content-strategist` — A skill that turns a product, audience, and goal into a 90-day content plan with prioritised topics, formats, and briefs.
2. [Long-form Writer](./skills/aipowered-long-form-writer/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-long-form-writer` — Turns a brief into a publishable 1,500-word piece — opinionated, specific, no AI-smell — with real examples, a clear thesis, and a single CTA at the end.
3. [LinkedIn Post Writer](./skills/aipowered-linkedin-post-writer/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-linkedin-post-writer` — Writes a single LinkedIn post in founder voice — three hooks to choose from, one CTA, no emoji theatre — that earns saves and replies, not vanity likes.

**Pro**
4. [Content Repurposer](./skills/aipowered-content-repurposer/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-content-repurposer` — Splits one long-form piece into 5 social posts, a newsletter section, and a video script — each tuned to the channel, not copy-pasted across them.
5. [Newsletter Writer](./skills/aipowered-newsletter-writer/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-newsletter-writer` — Drafts a weekly newsletter from raw inputs — wins, links, stories — that earns the open next week, not just this one, with a single voice and a single CTA.
6. [Keyword Clusterer](./skills/aipowered-keyword-clusterer/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-keyword-clusterer` — Groups raw keyword lists into pillar pages and supporting topics with intent and priority scoring, so a 2,000-keyword export becomes a 12-page content map.
7. [On-Page Optimiser](./skills/aipowered-on-page-optimiser/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-on-page-optimiser` — Audits a published URL against the SERP and search intent, returns a rewrite plan with internal-link suggestions, schema fixes, and a prioritised cut list.
8. [Twitter Thread Architect](./skills/aipowered-twitter-thread-architect/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-twitter-thread-architect` — Structures and writes a 7–12 tweet thread from one core idea, with each tweet earning the next read, a hook tested against three patterns, and one CTA at the end.
9. [Brand Voice Codifier](./skills/aipowered-brand-voice-codifier/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-brand-voice-codifier` — Extracts a usable voice guide from 5–10 writing samples — vocabulary, rhythm, forbidden words, and decision rules — that a junior writer can ship from on day one.
10. [Positioning Doctor](./skills/aipowered-positioning-doctor/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-positioning-doctor` — Diagnoses weak positioning in 30 minutes and returns a one-line statement of who-it's-for and what-it-replaces — testable on the homepage by tomorrow.
