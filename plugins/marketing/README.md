# Marketing

Plug-ins to plan, write, and ship content that compounds — from SEO and social to brand and lifecycle.

## When to install

You're running marketing for a product or service and want to:

- Build a content engine that doesn't depend on a copywriter for every asset
- Generate SEO-ready headlines, briefs, and outlines without the AI smell
- Keep tone and brand consistent across long-form, social, and lifecycle
- Move from "post and pray" to a measurable content strategy

## Categories

| Category | What lives here |
|---|---|
| `content` | Long-form, briefs, editorial calendars, repurposing |
| `seo` | Keyword research, headlines, on-page optimisation |
| `social` | LinkedIn, X, Instagram, threads, hooks |
| `brand` | Voice guides, positioning, messaging frameworks |
