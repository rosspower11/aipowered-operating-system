# Finance

Plug-ins for personal and business money — budgets, taxes, investing, and the financial machinery of running a company.

## When to install

You handle money for yourself or a business and want to:

- Build a budget you'll actually follow, not one that looks good on a spreadsheet
- Find tax-saving moves before year-end, not after
- Make investing decisions you can defend in a year
- Run a startup with a real runway model and pricing strategy

## Categories

| Category | What lives here |
|---|---|
| `cashflow` | Budgets, debt payoff, runway, expense management |
| `taxes` | Personal tax planning, business tax strategy, entity selection |
| `investing` | Portfolio review, retirement planning, wealth building |
| `business-finance` | Pricing, fundraising, financial modelling, dashboards |

## Skills

10 skills covering personal and business money. Three are `free`; seven are `pro` (members).

### Free

- [Budget Architect](./skills/aipowered-budget-architect/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-budget-architect` — `free`
  A skill that builds a personal or household budget that survives reality — fixed vs. flex spend, real categories, and a monthly ritual to review it without rage-quitting.
- [Debt Payoff Strategist](./skills/aipowered-debt-payoff-strategist/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-debt-payoff-strategist` — `free`
  A skill that turns a list of debts into an avalanche or snowball plan with real numbers, a payoff date, and the first 90 days of action — no vibes, just math.
- [Personal Tax Optimiser](./skills/aipowered-personal-tax-optimiser/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-personal-tax-optimiser` — `free`
  A skill that surfaces year-end personal tax-saving moves before December 31st — retirement contributions, HSA, charitable bunching, and tax-loss harvesting, with deadlines named.

### Pro

- [Runway Modeller](./skills/aipowered-runway-modeller/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-runway-modeller` — `pro`
  A skill that builds a startup runway model from MRR, headcount, and burn — names the cliff date and three real levers to extend it before the panic phase begins.
- [Business Tax Strategist](./skills/aipowered-business-tax-strategist/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-business-tax-strategist` — `pro`
  A skill that flags business tax-saving moves — entity selection (LLC/S-corp/C-corp), deductions, retirement plans, and R&D credit eligibility — without crossing into CPA territory.
- [Investment Portfolio Reviewer](./skills/aipowered-investment-portfolio-reviewer/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-investment-portfolio-reviewer` — `pro`
  A skill that reviews a portfolio against goals, risk tolerance, and tax efficiency — flags concentration risk, asset location problems, and the rebalance moves that matter.
- [Retirement Planner](./skills/aipowered-retirement-planner/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-retirement-planner` — `pro`
  A skill that models a retirement runway — savings rate vs. spending, withdrawal strategy, and the 5-year windows where small changes compound into entirely different outcomes.
- [Pricing Architect](./skills/aipowered-pricing-architect/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-pricing-architect` — `pro`
  A skill that sets product pricing using value-based, competitive, and willingness-to-pay analysis — with packaging tiers and the experiment plan to validate it in market.
- [Fundraising Financials Builder](./skills/aipowered-fundraising-financials-builder/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-fundraising-financials-builder` — `pro`
  A skill that builds the financial slides for a fundraising deck — historical, projection, use of funds — defendable in a partner meeting without spreadsheet panic.
- [Financial Dashboard Designer](./skills/aipowered-financial-dashboard-designer/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-financial-dashboard-designer` — `pro`
  A skill that designs a CFO/founder dashboard — the 8 metrics that actually drive decisions, refreshed weekly, with target bands and the early-warning thresholds that matter.
