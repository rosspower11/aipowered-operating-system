# Product

Plug-ins to ship better products faster — from research and PRDs to roadmap and design critique.

## When to install

You're running product (founder-PM, dedicated PM, or design-led) and want to:

- Turn raw research into clear synthesis the team can act on
- Write PRDs that engineers actually read instead of skimming
- Make roadmap trade-offs explicit, not political
- Critique design and copy without crushing the designer

## Categories

| Category | What lives here |
|---|---|
| `research` | User interviews, surveys, synthesis, jobs-to-be-done |
| `specs` | PRDs, RFCs, briefs, acceptance criteria |
| `roadmap` | Prioritisation, trade-offs, planning rituals |
| `design` | Design critique, copy review, UX patterns |

## Skills

### Free

- [PRD Writer](./skills/aipowered-prd-writer/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-prd-writer` — *free* — A skill that turns a one-line product idea into a tight, decision-ready PRD with scope, non-goals, open questions, and acceptance criteria.
- [User Researcher](./skills/aipowered-user-researcher/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-user-researcher` — *free* — Designs and scripts a 30-minute interview from a research hypothesis — questions ordered, biases flagged, recruit brief included.
- [Roadmap Prioritiser](./skills/aipowered-roadmap-prioritiser/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-roadmap-prioritiser` — *free* — Scores a backlog with RICE or WSJF, surfaces the top quarter, and names the trade-offs you're choosing to make.

### Pro

- [RFC Architect](./skills/aipowered-rfc-architect/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-rfc-architect` — *pro* — Turns an engineering decision into a tight RFC — context, options, recommendation, dissent — built for async review.
- [Launch Narrative Writer](./skills/aipowered-launch-narrative-writer/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-launch-narrative-writer` — *pro* — Drafts the internal narrative doc for a launch (Amazon-style) — press release, FAQ, and the strongest objection in writing.
- [Survey Designer](./skills/aipowered-survey-designer/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-survey-designer` — *pro* — Writes an unbiased survey question by question — defends each one, kills leading wording, sets the answer scale.
- [Jobs-to-be-Done Mapper](./skills/aipowered-jtbd-mapper/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-jtbd-mapper` — *pro* — Synthesises 5–10 interview transcripts into a jobs map — situations, motivations, outcomes, with quotes attached.
- [Quarterly Planner](./skills/aipowered-quarterly-planner/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-quarterly-planner` — *pro* — Runs an async quarterly planning session — gathers inputs, names trade-offs, lands on a sequenced plan you can ship.
- [Design Critic](./skills/aipowered-design-critic/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-design-critic` — *pro* — Critiques mocks against the goal and design heuristics — names what's broken, what's bikeshedding, and what to ship.
- [UX Copy Reviewer](./skills/aipowered-ux-copy-reviewer/SKILL.md) — `aipowered-ux-copy-reviewer` — *pro* — Reviews UI copy line by line — cuts dead words, fixes button verbs, surfaces hidden user assumptions.
